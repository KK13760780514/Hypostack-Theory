# The Objective-Neutral Rulebook (Structure Plan and Writing Guide)

> Status: **starter framework** (structure plan finalized; the body awaits community authorship)

## 1. Positioning and Principles

The objective-neutral version is **another document** equivalent in content to [玄叠论.md](玄叠论.md) (the original document is not modified). Its target reader is a researcher who does not accept the information-ontology presupposition but wants to evaluate the theory's logic and evidence.

Three principles:

1. **Presupposition–corollary–testing structure preserved**: the four presuppositions, five corollaries, nine-dimensional coordinates, ten-layer chain, testing standards, and honest boundaries are all preserved — their logical content unchanged.
2. **Narrative and self-reference removed**: the flyleaf rhetoric ("this is not a tool"), Part 9 self-referential demonstration, and first-person narration ("the proposer believes") are moved to an appendix or deleted.
3. **Assertions rewritten as testable statements**: "all systems evolve along the minimum-consumption path" → "under the operational definitions (E/N/S preregistered), the system's preference for lower-S paths constitutes a testable prediction; the current evidence level is L4_candidate".

## 3. Writing Rules

1. **No first person**: no "the proposer" or "we". Use "the theory asserts" / "per Presupposition 2".
2. **No value adjectives**: no "profound" or "revolutionary". Use checkable statements.
3. **Separate evidence from assertion**: label each assertion's evidence status (presupposition / corollary / L2 post-hoc translation / L4_candidate prediction).
4. **Explicitly list open problems**: point to [open-validation/open-decisions.md](open-validation/open-decisions.md) and [open-validation/math-foundations.md](open-validation/math-foundations.md).
5. **End each chapter with a "falsifiable items" list**: enumerate the concrete assertions in that chapter that an experiment could overturn.
