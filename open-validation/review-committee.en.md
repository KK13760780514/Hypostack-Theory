# HypoStack Empirical Review Committee (being formed)

> Goal: make the [ISSUE-006 anti-"calibration shopping" rules](known-issues.md) actually effective. The committee is not formed yet — reviews are currently performed by the proposer, creating a conflict-of-interest risk; forming the committee is the key step from "self-validation" toward "community consensus" in open validation.
> Status: **recruiting**. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to participate and [review-checklist.md](review-checklist.md) for review criteria. Recruitment announcement: [issue #10](https://github.com/KK13760780514/Hypostack-Theory/issues/10).

## 1. Responsibilities

1. **Reinstatement votes**: vote on requests to restore candidate status after a calibration change (≥2/3 agreement, corresponding to the ISSUE-006 cooldown/reinstatement threshold);
2. **Escalation review**: review submissions that challenge core assumptions or claim upgrades (see [review-checklist.md](review-checklist.md) section 6);
3. **Decision advice**: technical recommendations on [DEC-001~008](open-decisions.en.md) to help the community converge.

## 2. Eligibility (application-based)

Either of the following qualifies:

- At least 1 valid evidence submission in this repository (any category: support / challenge / falsification / exploratory);
- Or a verifiable professional background in a related discipline (physics / chemistry / biology / CS / economics / statistics).

**Conflict of interest**: members must not review their own (including co-authored) submissions and must declare this when reviewing.

## 3. Voting Rules

- Restore candidate status: **≥2/3** written agreement of committee members (with rationale, publicly recorded);
- Once the committee reaches 9 members, it may be reconsidered whether to raise the threshold to ≥4/5;
- Unresolved decisions are moved to [open-decisions.en.md](open-decisions.en.md) for community discussion, never shelved indefinitely.

## 4. How to Join

Open an issue stating your background and intent (the [task-proposal template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/task-proposal.md) works), labeled `Claiming committee member`; maintainers verify eligibility and add you to the member list in this file.
